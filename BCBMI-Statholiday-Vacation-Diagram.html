Stat Holiday Pay — Decision Flow

Implementation Business Rules | Bee-Clean / Findd | Feb 18, 2026
Core Definitions
Tier 1 (Reversible Leave) Standard paid leaves (Vacation, Sick, Personal, Wellness) that are removed from the stat date. The hours are credited back to the accrual balance. Employee receives Stat Pay instead of Leave Pay.
Tier 2 (Non-Reversible Leave) Circumstantial leaves (Bereavement, Jury Duty) where the deduction remains. These are treated as "elected days off" and do not trigger Stat Pay if they overlap a holiday.
Unpaid Leave Specifically defined as Unpaid Sick and Unpaid Leave. Employee is not paid for leave, does not receive stat, and the request remains deducted from balance.
Stat Pay Suppression Stat pay is not generated. Employee keeps leave deduction and is paid from their accrual balance at regular rate. Used to prevent double-pay at Open Sites.
Note on Wellness Leave Wellness follows Tier 1 path at closed sites but is suppressed at open sites (primarily YVR), following the same logic as sick/personal.
